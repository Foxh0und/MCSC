**We run a local membership in Melbourne for Chelsea Supporters.**

**Membership to our Official Melbourne Club for the 2026/27 runs from July 1st until mid-December.**

We have had a club set up here, with a higher level of organisation, since 2013. 
There are around 150 who buy the Melbourne membership each season and many have become good friends through meeting at club social functions and matchday events.


Our Official Supporters Club is a paid membership and if you live in Metro Melbourne it will cost you just $65. If you have family/household who are Chelsea Supporters too, then their fee is $45. 
It’s also $45 if you’re in Country Victoria or inter-state, and even international. 
These prices include the items being mailed to you. 

For *season 2026/27* we have included three MCSC branded items.
A toiletries bag, a string backpack and a multi-device charging cable which accommodates micro usb, usb C and older Apple devices. Our logo also lights up on it when it is in use. A club bumper sticker is always included, along with the MCSC membership card and badge for the season. Those last two items are in recognition of Matthew Harding as it’s the 30th anniversary of his untimely passing in 1996.   

![2026membership](/assets/2026membershippack.jpeg)


Being a member will also earn you a discount on drinks at our two venues that we use to watch matches (CQ Bar and Exford Hotel), so ensure you keep the card with you if you venture to the city.
CQ Bar offers discounts on non-matchdays too.

If you are keen to buy a membership, please download [this word doc](/assets/2026.27 Membership form_YOUR NAME.docx] and follow the instructions within, ensuring you email it to both club email addresses, as requested on the form. 

If you’re unsure of what price to pay due to your address, or number of friends/family you’d like to have join with you, then send an email to [admin@melbournechelseafcinmelbourne](mailto:admin@chelseafcinmelbourne.com) and we can clarify for you.


During the season we arrange meet ups for matches, if the kick off times are kind to us.
If you’ve been here in Melbourne for a while then you’ll know that usually the start and the end of the season when the time difference is 9 hours from the UK

We will also arrange the odd social function away from matches.
Those are always communicated via email and within a private Facebook group.

## Chelsea FC Membership
This is a membership directly with Chelsea FC. If you are hopeful to get across to London, then it is strongly advised that you buy this form of Chelsea membership, please check the [Chelsea FC website](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/chelsea-official-memberships). and also check the [Stamford Bridge Trips](https://www.chelseafcinmelbourne.com/stamfordbridge/) tab on this website.
